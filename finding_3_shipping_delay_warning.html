⚠ Shipping delayDue to increased demand, orders may take up to 30 days to ship.
ProblemThe prominent shipping delay warning at the top of the homepage immediately triggers a negative emotional response, creating hesitation before the user even sees the value proposition. This warning is presented without context or mitigation, making the delay seem like a major flaw rather than a temporary issue. As a result, users may abandon the site before exploring products, leading to lost conversions.
RECOMMENDED — Softened message

Homepage Hero

Wildly Comfortable. Super Natural.

Experience the comfort of nature with our premium products.

Shop All
✓ Free shippingFree shipping on orders over $100. Note: some orders may take up to 30 days due to high demand. We'll keep you updated.
FixSoften the shipping message by leading with a positive incentive (free shipping) and framing the delay as a note rather than a warning. Add a reassurance that the company will keep the customer updated, which reduces anxiety. This maintains transparency while minimizing the negative impact on user trust and purchase intent.
